Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026. Contact: itsroydob@gmail.com.

What Open Chat is

Open Chat is a messaging app for Android, currently in a private pilot. This policy describes what data the app handles during the pilot.

Account data

If you create an account, we store your email address and login credentials (passwords are stored as irreversible hashes, never in readable form) so you can sign in. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo reference from Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Profile and contacts

Your display name, username, and about line are stored on our backend (Supabase) so your contacts can find and recognize you. Contact invitations, connections, and blocks are stored for the same purpose. Your profile photo stays on your device during the pilot and is not uploaded.

Messages

During the pilot, conversations shown in the app are a local on-device demo used for interface testing; they are not sent to other people. Real message delivery is being built on the Signal protocol with keys generated and kept on your device. This policy will be updated before real messaging launches.

Security data on your device

Encryption keys are generated on your phone and protected by the Android Keystore. Private keys never leave your device.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, upload your address book, or share your information with third parties except the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (currently Supabase for accounts and Google for sign-in).

Your choices

You can edit your name, about line, and photo in the app, block contacts at any time, and sign out. To delete your account and data during the pilot, email itsroydob@gmail.com from your account address.

Changes

We will update this page as features launch, starting with real encrypted messaging.
